# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in Pit welcoming and harassment-free for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and specific.
- Assume good intent while taking responsibility for impact.
- Welcome questions and differing technical perspectives.
- Keep criticism focused on ideas and code, not people.
- Protect private information and respect coordinated security disclosure.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, sustained disruption, deliberate exposure of private information, and other conduct that makes participation unsafe are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior violates this policy. Reports will be reviewed fairly and kept as confidential as practical.

To report conduct concerns privately, contact the repository owner through the contact options on https://github.com/cv. Do not use a public issue for a sensitive report.

This policy applies in project spaces and when representing the project elsewhere.
